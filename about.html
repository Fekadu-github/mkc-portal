WELCOME

Welcome to Meserete Kristos Church — Amanuel Addis Ababa Local Church. We are an Evangelical Christian church serving the Akaki Kality community in Addis Ababa. Our desire is to exalt Jesus Christ, teach the Word of God, build believers in faith, and reach people with the Gospel.
